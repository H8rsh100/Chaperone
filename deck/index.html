Aegis-Swarm

Tracks context across scripted roles, isolates the executor on a taint match, and gates the action itself. Fail-closed. Recorded.

Inside the kernel: four layers, one decision
rules µs→ embeddings MiniLM 384-d→ LLM judge off hot-path→ provenance sentinel taint→ policy allow / review / deny→ execution allowlist→ hash-chained ledger

Deterministic first. Local model with a preloaded cache. A sentinel matches markers and overlap from observed sources. Human review and an allowlist are backstops.

Aegis-Swarm: the layer above the kernel

Follow the data, not just the command.

planner trusted→ retriever untrusted fetch→ taint graph lineage→ saliency analysis evidence→ Chaperone kernel fail-closed→ isolation match carry ≥ 0.5→ Ed25519 ledger

Scripted planner, retriever, executor; fixed command. The kernel blocks on a canary match; Aegis isolates the executor. A human can keep it isolated or resume on a sanitized context.
